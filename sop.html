Procedure 2.1: Commercial Lead Entry, 10 Standard Sources & 8-Stage Funnel

Open Leads & Pipeline (leads.html) and click + Add New Lead. Follow these mandatory entry rules:

  • Lead Owner: Auto-fills to the logged-in user creating the lead — never entered manually.
  • Lead Source, Industry Vertical, Project Sector / Origin, Vertical Classification, Currency: All five are picked from live Master Data lists (master-data.html) rather than hardcoded options. Admins/leadership can add, edit, or delete entries in these lists at any time without a code change, and every lead across the org immediately sees the updated list.
  • Vertical & Cascading Product Specs: Choose Industry Vertical and Project Sector first — the Product Name dropdown then dynamically filters to only matching items from the Product Master. Selecting a product auto-populates HSN Code, Technical Specification, and Price as read-only fields — never typed in manually, so pricing always matches the master catalog.
  • Multi-Contact Entry: Add up to 5 customer contact persons with Designation, Mobile, Email, and Auto-CC toggles.
  • 8-Stage Funnel Progression: Maintain active progression through:
    1. Customer Contacted / Contact Attempted
    2. Follow-Up Scheduled
    3. Site Visit Completed
    4. Technical Evaluation in Progress
    5. Commercial Offer Submitted (Auto-synced from Quotation)
    6. Pre-Qualification Completed (Eligibility confirmed)
    7. Lead Qualified (Confirmed sales opportunity with high order probability)
    8. Order Confirmed (Won - Ready for PO / Order Booking)

Procedure 2.2: Quotation Creation, Lead Linkage & 2-Stage Approval

  • Link to Lead (CRM): Search and select the source Lead by number, customer, or vertical — Customer, Contact Person, Email/Mobile, and Vertical auto-fill from the Lead and lock (edit the Lead itself, or unlink to "Standalone Customer", to change them).
  • Duplicate-quote prevention: A Lead that already has an active (non-rejected) quotation cannot have a second one raised against it — edit or delete the existing quote first.
  • Mandatory CC: Every quotation email automatically CCs measuredichennai@gmail.com — this is enforced by the system and cannot be removed.
  • Discounts can be entered as absolute value (₹) with auto % calculation. Up to 10 PDF/ZIP attachments and delivery commitment lead-time are supported. A First Advance % is captured on the quote (defaults 50%) and later flows straight into the Order — see 2.3.

Every quotation — regardless of discount level — goes through the same 2-stage sign-off before it can be emailed to the client:

Stage Designated Authority Blocking? System Enforcement
1. Primary Approval The employee flagged "Primary Approver" Yes — blocking Quotation cannot be emailed to the client until this sign-off happens, for every discount level.
2. Director Ratification The employee flagged "Director" No — non-blocking Recorded for the record after the fact; does not hold up sending the quote or booking the order.

Pending-approval quotations always sort to the top of the registry. Already-invoiced quotations are hidden from the default list (still reachable via search or the "Show already-invoiced" toggle) to keep the working list short.

Procedure 2.3: Order Booking, Quote-Locked Valuation & 100% Sales Contribution Split

  • Open Orders & Contracts (orders.html). Select the Customer / Account first — the Quotation dropdown then shows only that customer's Approved (and not already-booked) quotations, never a Pending/Rejected/Draft one and never a free-text entry.
  • Everything locks to the Quotation: Order Value, GST rate/amount, Total, and 1st Advance % & amount are pulled straight from the selected quote and shown read-only — they cannot be typed in or overridden. If the customer's real PO doesn't match, the fix is either to get the PO corrected by the client, or to edit the Quotation itself to match the PO and re-select it — the app will not save a mismatched order.
  • Mandatory 100% Contribution Split: Add all contributing sales engineers and allocate percentage effort (summing to exactly 100%) — entered manually, not auto-filled. This split now correctly drives each contributor's revenue in AOP / my-scorecard.html / my-team.html once the order is booked.
  • Bank / Performance Guarantee (BG/PG) amount and expiry date are entered manually if required by the customer contract.
  • Order confirmation follows the same 2-stage sign-off as quotations (Primary Approver blocking, Director ratification non-blocking) before it counts as "Booked" and reconciles the linked Lead.
  • Already-invoiced orders are hidden from the default list (reachable via search or "Show already-invoiced").

Procedure 2.4: Finance Department Commercial Invoicing & AR Governance

  • Role Restriction: Commercial Invoices (Tax & Proforma) are raised strictly by the Accounting and Finance Department (and Director / Super Admin). Sales engineers can view invoice statuses and balance dues.
  • Invoices auto-populate line items and GST details directly from confirmed POs.
  • Every invoice (no value threshold) requires the same 2-stage sign-off as quotations/orders — Primary Approver blocking, Director ratification non-blocking — before it can be dispatched to the client.
  • Official invoices feature company bank details from bankDetailsMaster and the authorized signatory seal and digital signature of Mr. Ravichandran (Director).
  • All activities across Leads, Quotes, Orders, Invoices, and Master Data are logged into the Universal Audit Trail viewable exclusively by the Director and Super Admin.

Procedure 2.5: Approval Authority Model & the "✅ Approvals" Hub

  • Three named, assignable authority flags drive every approval gate in the app: Primary Approver, Director Ratifier, and Finance Head. They are assigned per employee on the Employees page ("Approval Authority" section on Add/Edit) — not hardcoded to any one person.
  • Mr. Ravichandran (Director) holds universal approval authority — as the ultimate authority of the organization, his sign-off satisfies any approval gate (Primary Approver, Finance Head, or Director) across the entire app, so nothing can ever get stuck waiting on someone else if he needs to act.
  • Anyone holding at least one of these three flags sees a standalone "✅ Approvals" menu item in the top navigation, with a live count badge. This is the single place to see everything awaiting your sign-off — quotations, orders, invoices, payment verifications, and write-off/goodwill requests — grouped by category, with a direct link into the source record to act on it.

Finance, Invoicing & Accounts Receivable (AR) SOP

Standard operating guidelines for GST tax billing, 2-stage invoice approval, Finance Head payment verification, and 3-way write-off/goodwill sign-off.

Procedure 3.1: Tax & Proforma Invoice Issuance

  1. Open Invoices & Approvals (invoices.html) and click Create New Invoice.
  2. Select Client, Project, and Invoice Type (Tax Invoice or Proforma Invoice).
  3. Line items auto-calculate 18% GST (CGST+SGST 9%+9% for intra-state Karnataka, or IGST 18% for inter-state).
  4. Every invoice enters Pending Senior Approval regardless of value, and requires the Primary Approver's sign-off (blocking dispatch) followed by Director ratification (non-blocking) — see Procedure 2.5.
  5. When client pays against Proforma, use Convert to Tax Invoice to generate official tax invoice number without re-entering data.

Procedure 3.2: Payment Logging & Finance Head Verification

  1. Open Payments & Collections (payments.html) upon receipt of bank credit.
  2. Select invoice from the pending collection dropdown (automatically calculates outstanding balance).
  3. Enter Bank Transaction Reference (UTR / Cheque), TDS Deducted (if any), and Net Amount Received — classify as Advance, Part Payment, or Final Payment.
  4. Finance Head verification gate: every payment (advance, part, or final) requires sign-off from the employee flagged "Finance Head" before it counts as recognized revenue anywhere in the app — dashboards, AOP achievement, and financial statements only reflect verified payments.

Procedure 3.3: Request Adjustment / Write-Off (Goodwill or Bad Debt)

  1. From Payments & Collections, click + Request Adjustment / Write-Off.
  2. Filter Client (Pending AR Only): the client dropdown lists strictly only clients who currently have a real outstanding balance — never a fully-settled account.
  3. Select Pending Invoice: once a client is chosen, the invoice dropdown cascades to show only that client's pending invoices.
  4. Choose adjustment type — Goodwill Discount / Commercial Adjustment or Bad Debt Write-Off (Unrecoverable AR) — enter the amount and a mandatory commercial justification.
  5. 3-Way Sign-Off: the request appears in the pending-approval list immediately with its full amount, and requires all three named authorities — Primary Approver, Finance Head, and Director — to sign off independently. It only takes effect once all three have approved.
  6. Financial impact once approved: the invoice balance reduces immediately, and the approved amount flows automatically into the company's Profit & Loss (reduces Net Profit/EBITDA), Balance Sheet (reduces Trade Receivables), and Cash Flow Statement (disclosed as a non-cash memo item) on the Finance → Financial Statements page.

Service & Field Engineering Team SOP

Operating procedures for breakdown tickets, calibration SLAs, spare parts requisitions, and emergency callouts.

1. Ticket Logging & SLA Triage

When a client reports an equipment fault or calibration request:

  • Open service-tickets.html and click Raise Ticket.
  • Set Priority: P1 Critical (≤4h SLA), P2 High (≤12h SLA), P3 Medium (≤24h), or P4 Low.
  • Assign Lead Engineer based on client site territory.

2. On-Site Action & Parts Usage

During client site troubleshooting:

  • Punch Attendance with Site / On-Field location flag.
  • Log DWM task as Special Assignment - Client Emergency Breakdown.
  • Record spare part replacements (load cells, indicators, junction boxes) from Master Catalog.

3. Ticket Closure & Sign-Off

Post-restoration and customer verification:

  • Upload service report notes and root cause analysis (RCA).
  • Change ticket status to Resolved → Closed with customer sign-off.
  • Trigger Service Quotation or Billing Invoice if billable out-of-warranty.

HR, Attendance, Payroll & Appraisal Governance SOP

Standard operating cadence for headcount administration, payroll cutoffs, Loss-Of-Pay deductions, and quarterly appraisals.

Procedure 6.1: Monthly Payroll Processing & Loss-Of-Pay (LOP)

  1. Payroll cutoff date is the 25th of every month.
  2. HR opens Payroll & CTC (payroll.html) to review unapproved leave records and missing punch logs.
  3. The system automatically calculates Loss-Of-Pay (LOP) deductions: (Monthly Gross Salary / 30) * Unapproved Absent Days.
  4. Statutory deductions (Provident Fund 12%, ESI 0.75%, TDS) are computed and net disbursement vouchers are locked for Director sign-off.

Procedure 6.2: Quarterly Performance Appraisals & Reviews

  1. At quarter end (Q1 to Q4), employee fills self-assessment on Performance Reviews (reviews.html).
  2. Reporting Manager evaluates target metrics vs actual output logged in My Scorecard and DWM.
  3. Manager provides numerical ratings (1.0 to 5.0 scale) and qualitative growth goals.
  4. Managing Director conducts final calibration review for annual compensation increments and promotions.
