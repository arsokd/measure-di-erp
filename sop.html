Day 1 Onboarding & Daily Routine SOP (Every Employee)

Standard operating cadence for attendance, daily task commitments, special assignments, and scorecards.

09:00 AM - 09:30 AM attendance.html

Step 1: Attendance Check-In & Work Arrangement

Navigate to Attendance & Leaves on your web or mobile device.

  • Select work location: Office / In-Station, Site / On-Field, or Work-From-Home (WFH).
  • Click Punch In to capture the timestamp and start shift tracking.
  • If applying for leave or half-day, submit via the Apply Leave form at least 24h in advance.
09:30 AM - 10:00 AM dwm.html

Step 2: Daily Work Management (DWM) Morning Plan

Navigate to Daily Work (DWM). Your employee ID is auto-populated from session.

  • Log planned task deliverables with allocated hours totaling 8.0 hrs standard.
  • Link tasks to specific Customer Leads, Projects, or Service Tickets.
  • Special Assignment Rule: If attending full-day customer training, installation audit, or emergency breakdown, select Special Assignment to receive 100% full-day productivity credit.
06:00 PM - 06:30 PM dwm.html / my-scorecard.html

Step 3: Evening Actuals & Punch-Out

Close your workday by confirming task accomplishment status and checkout time.

  • Update task status: Done, Partial, or Rescheduled with brief remarks.
  • Review daily productivity score gauge (≥85% is target green).
  • Return to Attendance and click Punch Out to record total shift duration.
  • Check My Scorecard weekly to monitor achievement pace across active KRAs.

Procedure 2.1: Commercial Lead Entry, 10 Standard Sources & 8-Stage Funnel

Open Leads & Pipeline (leads.html) and click + Add New Lead. Follow these mandatory entry rules:

  • Lead Source (Select from 10 Prescribed Channels): India Mart, Tender / E-Procurement Portal, SEO, Existing Client, Customer Reference, Ariba, OEM, Mail Marketing / Digital Marketing, Direct Customer Approach, or Exhibition / Trade Fair.
  • Industry Origin: Select from Onboard, Project (specify sector: Steel, Cement, Power Plant, Infrastructure, Mining, Other), Crane, or Spare/Service.
  • Vertical & Cascading Product Specs: Choose Vertical (Projects, Onboard, Crane, Service and Parts). The product dropdown will dynamically filter only items belonging to that vertical from the Product Master, auto-filling technical specs and HSN codes.
  • Multi-Contact Entry: Add up to 5 customer contact persons with Designation, Mobile, Email, and Auto-CC toggles.
  • 8-Stage Funnel Progression: Maintain active progression through:
    1. Customer Contacted / Contact Attempted
    2. Follow-Up Scheduled
    3. Site Visit Completed
    4. Technical Evaluation in Progress
    5. Commercial Offer Submitted (Auto-synced from Quotation)
    6. Pre-Qualification Completed (Eligibility confirmed)
    7. Lead Qualified (Confirmed sales opportunity with high order probability)
    8. Order Confirmed (Won - Ready for PO / Order Booking)

Procedure 2.2: Quotation Generation & 2-Tier Approval Workflow

Quotation pricing and gross margins follow a strict 2-tier approval hierarchy:

Approval Level Designated Authority Threshold Condition System Enforcement
Level 1 Mr. Murugan V (Sales & Marketing Head) Standard Quotations (≤25% Discount) Mandatory technical & commercial review before client email dispatch.
Level 2 Mr. Ravichandran (Director) Discount Exceeds 25.0% Escalated to Director sign-off. Quotation locked until approved.

Discounts can be entered as absolute value (₹) with auto % calculation. Up to 10 PDF/ZIP attachments and delivery commitment lead-time are supported.

Procedure 2.3: Order Booking & 100% Sales Contribution Split

  • Open Orders & Contracts (orders.html) and link the approved Quotation.
  • Mandatory 100% Contribution Split: Add all contributing sales engineers and allocate percentage effort (summing to exactly 100%). This feeds individual AOP revenue achievement in my-scorecard.html.
  • Attach signed Customer PO Copy and Technical Factory Instruction Document.
  • Configure Bank / Performance Guarantee (BG/PG) amount and expiry date if required by customer contract.

Procedure 2.4: Finance Department Commercial Invoicing & AR Governance

  • Role Restriction: Commercial Invoices (Tax & Proforma) are raised strictly by the Accounting and Finance Department (and Director / Super Admin). Sales engineers can view invoice statuses and balance dues.
  • Invoices auto-populate line items and GST details directly from confirmed POs.
  • Official invoices feature company bank details from bankDetailsMaster and the authorized signatory seal and digital signature of Mr. Ravichandran (Director).
  • All activities across Leads, Quotes, Orders, Invoices, and Master Data are logged into the Universal Audit Trail viewable exclusively by the Director and Super Admin.

Finance, Invoicing & Accounts Receivable (AR) SOP

Standard operating guidelines for GST tax billing, Director approval gates, collection reconciliation, and bad debt adjustments.

Procedure 3.1: Tax & Proforma Invoice Issuance

  1. Open Invoices & Approvals (invoices.html) and click Create New Invoice.
  2. Select Client, Project, and Invoice Type (Tax Invoice or Proforma Invoice).
  3. Line items auto-calculate 18% GST (CGST+SGST 9%+9% for intra-state Karnataka, or IGST 18% for inter-state).
  4. Director Approval Threshold: Invoices with total value ≥ INR 5,00,000 enter Pending Director Approval. Only Director / Super Admin can approve and release for client email dispatch.
  5. When client pays against Proforma, use Convert to Tax Invoice to generate official tax invoice number without re-entering data.

Procedure 3.2: Payment Logging & Director AR Write-Offs

  1. Open Payments & Collections (payments.html) upon receipt of bank credit.
  2. Select invoice from the pending collection dropdown (automatically calculates outstanding balance).
  3. Enter Bank Transaction Reference (UTR / Cheque), TDS Deducted (if any), and Net Amount Received.
  4. Goodwill Discount / Bad Debt Write-Off Rule:
    • If a customer dispute or balance rounding requires an adjustment, click Request AR Adjustment.
    • Enter Adjustment Type (Goodwill Discount or Bad Debt Write-Off) with mandatory audit explanation.
    • Write-offs are locked until approved by Managing Director to prevent unauthorized revenue leakage.

Service & Field Engineering Team SOP

Operating procedures for breakdown tickets, calibration SLAs, spare parts requisitions, and emergency callouts.

1. Ticket Logging & SLA Triage

When a client reports an equipment fault or calibration request:

  • Open service-tickets.html and click Raise Ticket.
  • Set Priority: P1 Critical (≤4h SLA), P2 High (≤12h SLA), P3 Medium (≤24h), or P4 Low.
  • Assign Lead Engineer based on client site territory.

2. On-Site Action & Parts Usage

During client site troubleshooting:

  • Punch Attendance with Site / On-Field location flag.
  • Log DWM task as Special Assignment - Client Emergency Breakdown.
  • Record spare part replacements (load cells, indicators, junction boxes) from Master Catalog.

3. Ticket Closure & Sign-Off

Post-restoration and customer verification:

  • Upload service report notes and root cause analysis (RCA).
  • Change ticket status to Resolved → Closed with customer sign-off.
  • Trigger Service Quotation or Billing Invoice if billable out-of-warranty.

HR, Attendance, Payroll & Appraisal Governance SOP

Standard operating cadence for headcount administration, payroll cutoffs, Loss-Of-Pay deductions, and quarterly appraisals.

Procedure 6.1: Monthly Payroll Processing & Loss-Of-Pay (LOP)

  1. Payroll cutoff date is the 25th of every month.
  2. HR opens Payroll & CTC (payroll.html) to review unapproved leave records and missing punch logs.
  3. The system automatically calculates Loss-Of-Pay (LOP) deductions: (Monthly Gross Salary / 30) * Unapproved Absent Days.
  4. Statutory deductions (Provident Fund 12%, ESI 0.75%, TDS) are computed and net disbursement vouchers are locked for Director sign-off.

Procedure 6.2: Quarterly Performance Appraisals & Reviews

  1. At quarter end (Q1 to Q4), employee fills self-assessment on Performance Reviews (reviews.html).
  2. Reporting Manager evaluates target metrics vs actual output logged in My Scorecard and DWM.
  3. Manager provides numerical ratings (1.0 to 5.0 scale) and qualitative growth goals.
  4. Managing Director conducts final calibration review for annual compensation increments and promotions.
