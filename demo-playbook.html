🎤 Client Demo Playbook

Your run-of-show for presenting Measure DI RevOps professionally — what to click, what to say, what to highlight, in order.

✅ 1. Pre-Demo Checklist

  • Log in fresh (log out and back in) so the session isn't carrying stale test edits from a previous practice run.
  • Decide which login to demo from — Director (Ravichandran) shows every menu including Dashboard, Approvals, Reports, Payroll; a staff login is only worth using if the client specifically wants to see role restrictions in action.
  • Confirm real data is in place: open My Scorecard and AOP Targets at FY 2026-27 and check the real ₹20 Cr company target and the 8-person KRA cascade are showing (not the old placeholder numbers).
  • Check your internet connection — live Firestore sync needs it; a flaky connection mid-demo will show a "saved locally" banner that's confusing to explain live.
  • Pick one client story to walk end-to-end (a Lead that became a Quote, an Order, an Invoice, a Payment) rather than jumping between unrelated random records — a connected story is far more convincing than isolated screens.
  • Close any other browser tabs with admin tools, GitHub, or Netlify open — screen-share hygiene.

🗺️ 2. Suggested Demo Flow, In Order

Follow the app's real top-nav order — it reads as one coherent system, not a list of disconnected features.

📊 Open on: Executive Dashboard

Say: "Everything you'll see today rolls up to this one screen — pipeline conversion, revenue vs target, ratio-scaled across the week, month, quarter, or full year." Switch the FY selector once (2024-25 → 2026-27) to show multi-year history isn't a one-off — it's structural.

✅ Approvals Hub

Say: "Approval authority is assigned to named people, not job titles — so it's exactly who you choose, and one person can hold it all if needed." Point at the live pending-count badge in the nav bar before opening it.

🗄️ Master Data (quick touch, don't dwell)

Say: "Every dropdown across the app — clients, products, spare parts, service SLA tiers — comes from here, so a change here reflects everywhere instantly. Editing it is restricted to named people you choose, enforced at the database level, not just hidden on screen." Open one list (e.g. AMC Contract Tier) to show it's genuinely editable, not hardcoded.

📈 Sales & Revenue — walk ONE client's full story here

  1. Leads: show the 8-stage funnel and that Source/Vertical/Sector are picked from Master Data, not typed.
  2. Quotations: lead-locks to the chosen customer, one active quote per lead (try adding a duplicate to show it's blocked), 2-stage sign-off before it can even be emailed — first by Subhashini/Dipa/Anitha, then non-blocking final approval by Murugan or the Director. Good talking point: if one of the three seniors raises their own quote, it's approved instantly, no sign-off needed — only their team's records go through both stages.
  3. Orders — the differentiator, don't rush this: click "Book New Order," pick the customer, show the "Pending Approved Quotation" dropdown only lists that customer's approved quotes.   4. Invoices: every invoice, any value, same 2-stage gate before client dispatch.
  5. Payments & Write-Offs — good "wow" moment: open a pending-AR client, show the invoice dropdown is strictly limited to that client's actual pending invoices. Then open a write-off/goodwill request already awaiting approval, approve it live if you can, and immediately flip to Expenses → P&L / Balance Sheet / Cash Flow to show the number move in real time.

⭐ 3. Recently Built — Don't Let These Get Lost in the Flow

Easy to skip past quickly if you're moving fast. Slow down for these specifically — they're the newest, most differentiating work.

  • Quote-locked order booking — an Order's valuation/tax/advance cannot mismatch its source Quote. Show the rejection, not just the happy path.
  • Sales Contribution Split → Target Achievement — the manual split is the only manual figure; it drives real KRA/AOP achievement automatically.
  • AR write-off → live P&L / Balance Sheet / Cash Flow — approval isn't just a status change, it visibly moves the financial statements.
  • Master Data restricted authority — enforced in the database (Firestore rules), not just a hidden button. Mention this if asked about data governance/security.
  • Service & Quality's 3-vertical grouping — a structural, not cosmetic, reorganization around how the client actually generates revenue.
  • Real KRA/AOP cascade — sourced from the client's own sheet, re-keyed onto correct employees, cross-verified to the rupee.

❓ 4. Anticipated Questions & Suggested Answers

"What if two people both need to approve the same thing?"

Multiple people can hold the same authority flag — any one of them clears the gate; it doesn't require all of them unless it's specifically a 3-way sign-off item like a write-off.

"Can we change who has approval or Master Data authority later?"

Yes — it's a checkbox per employee on the Employee Directory page, takes effect immediately, no code change.

"Where does our data actually live? Is it backed up?"

Google Firebase/Firestore — the same infrastructure Google's own products run on. Every record is timestamped and versioned in the Audit Trail.

"What happens if the internet drops while someone's working?"

The app is local-first — it keeps working offline and syncs automatically once the connection returns; a banner tells the user if something couldn't sync yet.

"Can we get this data out into Excel or a PDF for our own records?"

Yes — Reports has one-click PDF/CSV exports, and the User Guide itself compiles into a live PDF on demand.
